Who does the mission help?

Children and families throughout Western North Carolina, including families affected by Hurricane Helene and residents with disabilities, as well as children receiving care at Levine Children’s Hospital. Support includes toys and gifts, community Christmas celebrations, shelters, wheelchair ramps and essential supplies.

Where can I donate toys or supplies?

Contact Todd for current collection locations, wish lists and deadlines. Send an email or call (803) 242-3135.

Can my business or group get involved?

Yes. The mission welcomes partners who give toys, fund toys and outreach supplies, host collection drives, help spread the word or volunteer. Connect with Todd to discuss what your group can contribute.

Where can I learn more about the mission?

Read our story and our mission, explore the photo album or see WCCB’s news coverage.
