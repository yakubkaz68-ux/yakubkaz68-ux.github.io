Website font notices

The website serves its fonts locally through Astro and Fontsource. This folder
includes the unmodified SIL Open Font License 1.1 notices for the font families
configured in astro.config.ts. Inter is retained for the underlying template;
the mission pages use Berkshire Swash, Lora and DM Sans.

Berkshire Swash: berkshire-swash-OFL.txt (existing notice)
https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/berkshireswash/OFL.txt

Lora: lora-OFL.txt
https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/lora/OFL.txt

DM Sans: dmsans-OFL.txt
https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/dmsans/OFL.txt

Inter: inter-OFL.txt
https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/inter/OFL.txt
